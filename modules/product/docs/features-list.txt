Core Identity & Pricing
[ ] Lifecycle Control: Manage product states by creating, updating, drafting, publishing, and archiving core records.
[ ] Catalog visibility: Show in catalog and search, catalog only, search only, or hidden (WooCommerce visibility).
[ ] Featured: Flag a product for home and merchandising lists.
[ ] Identifiers & Details: Titles, short and long descriptions, brands, and identifiers including SKU, barcode, GTIN, UPC, EAN, ISBN, and MPN.
[ ] Pricing Structure: Base retail price, compare-at / sale price, sale start and end (UTC), internal cost, tax status and tax class.
[ ] Product Types: Physical (ships), virtual (no shipping), downloadable (files), grouped, bundles / kits, and affiliate / external URLs.
[ ] Shipping catalog data: Weight, length / width / height, and shipping class. No rate quotes here.
[ ] Sold individually: Limit to one of this product per order (catalog flag, not stock).

Variations & Media
[ ] Multi-Option Variants: Individual SKU, barcode, and price for every option combination, plus a default variant.
[ ] Attributes & Specifications: Reusable specs, visual swatches, and facet flags for storefront filters.
[ ] Media Management: Image sets, primary thumbnail, reorder, map photos to variants, digital downloads with download limit and expiry.

Organization, SEO & Operations
[ ] Categorization: Nested category trees, tags, and manual or automated collections.
[ ] SEO Optimization: URL slugs, meta titles and descriptions, and slug redirects.
[ ] Relationships: Related items, up-sells, cross-sells, and frequently bought together (manual links).
[ ] Operations: Admin list, duplicate product, bulk edit, and CSV import / export.

Out of scope
[-] Stock, warehouses, reservations, backorders
[-] Coupons, sale campaigns, customer prices
[-] Cart, checkout, payments, tax rates
[-] Download entitlement after purchase
[-] Reviews
[-] Shipping quotes
[-] Search engine
[-] Translations
