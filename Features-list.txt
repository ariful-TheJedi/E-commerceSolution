E-Commerce Solution — Must-Have Feature List
Target: Small & Mid-Size Businesses (SMB)
Goal: Deliver a complete, reliable store that merchants can run without heavy IT support.

================================================================================
1. STOREFRONT (CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE)
================================================================================
- Responsive storefront (mobile-first; desktop + tablet)
- Home page with banners, featured products, collections
- Product listing with filters (price, category, brand, attributes)
- Product search with autocomplete / suggestions
- Product detail page (images gallery, variants, stock status, reviews)
- Wishlist / save for later
- Recently viewed products
- Related / “you may also like” products
- Multi-language & multi-currency support (optional modules)
- SEO-friendly URLs, meta titles/descriptions, sitemap, robots.txt
- Fast page load (caching, image optimization, CDN-ready)

================================================================================
2. CATALOG & INVENTORY
================================================================================
- Categories & subcategories (nested)
- Product types: simple, variant (size/color), digital, bundle
- SKU management
- Product images & media gallery
- Stock tracking (in stock / low stock / out of stock)
- Low-stock alerts for merchants
- Bulk product import/export (CSV/Excel)
- Product tags & collections
- Barcode / QR support (helpful for physical shops)
- Tax classes & tax rules by region

================================================================================
3. CART & CHECKOUT
================================================================================
- Add to cart / update quantity / remove items
- Guest checkout + registered-user checkout
- Coupon / discount codes (percentage, fixed, free shipping)
- Shipping method selection (flat rate, weight-based, zone-based)
- Delivery address book & billing address
- Order summary with taxes & fees
- Checkout progress clarity (few steps, clear errors)
- Abandoned cart recovery (email/SMS reminders)
- Order confirmation page + email/SMS receipt

================================================================================
4. PAYMENTS
================================================================================
- Multiple payment gateways (card, mobile wallet, bank transfer, COD)
- Cash on Delivery (COD) with optional confirmation rules
- Partial payments / deposits (useful for custom orders)
- Refunds & partial refunds from admin
- Payment status tracking (pending, paid, failed, refunded)
- Secure payment handling (PCI-aware integration; no raw card storage)
- Invoice / receipt generation (PDF)

================================================================================
5. ORDERS & FULFILLMENT
================================================================================
- Order lifecycle: Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered → Cancelled
- Order details view for customer and admin
- Order notes / internal merchant notes
- Shipping label / tracking number support
- Split shipments (partial fulfill)
- Return / RMA requests (reason, status, refund or exchange)
- Print packing slip / invoice
- Order search & filters (date, status, customer, payment method)

================================================================================
6. CUSTOMER ACCOUNTS
================================================================================
- Registration / login (email + optional phone OTP)
- Password reset / secure auth
- Customer profile & address management
- Order history & reorder
- Wishlist sync across devices
- Loyalty points / rewards (optional but high value for SMB retention)
- Customer support tickets or contact form linked to order

================================================================================
7. MERCHANT ADMIN PANEL
================================================================================
- Dashboard overview (sales, orders, visitors, low stock)
- Product / category / inventory CRUD
- Order management workspace
- Customer management
- Coupon & promotion management
- Shipping zones & rates configuration
- Tax & store settings
- Role-based staff access (Owner, Manager, Support, Warehouse)
- Activity / audit log for staff actions
- Store branding settings (logo, colors, banners)

================================================================================
8. MARKETING & GROWTH
================================================================================
- Discount campaigns (flash sale, BOGO, category sale)
- Banner / slider / homepage campaign manager
- Email marketing basics (newsletter signup, order emails)
- SMS notifications (order placed, shipped, delivered)
- Referral / affiliate codes (optional)
- Product reviews & ratings moderation
- Social share links
- Abandoned cart campaigns
- SEO tools (canonical tags, Open Graph, structured data / schema)

================================================================================
9. ANALYTICS & REPORTING
================================================================================
- Sales reports (daily/weekly/monthly)
- Best-selling products
- Revenue by category / payment method
- Customer acquisition & repeat purchase metrics
- Inventory valuation / stock movement report
- Coupon performance report
- Export reports (CSV/PDF)
- Basic funnel: views → add to cart → checkout → purchase

================================================================================
10. NOTIFICATIONS & COMMUNICATION
================================================================================
- Transactional emails (signup, order, shipping, refund)
- SMS / WhatsApp order updates (market-dependent)
- In-app admin notifications for new orders & low stock
- Customizable email/SMS templates

================================================================================
11. SHIPPING & LOGISTICS
================================================================================
- Shipping zones (city / region / country)
- Rate rules (flat, weight, price threshold)
- Free shipping thresholds
- Local pickup option
- Courier / delivery partner integration hooks
- Estimated delivery date display
- Real-time or manual tracking updates

================================================================================
12. SECURITY, TRUST & COMPLIANCE
================================================================================
- HTTPS everywhere
- Secure authentication (hashed passwords, session protection)
- Role-based permissions & least privilege
- CAPTCHA / bot protection on forms
- Rate limiting on login & checkout APIs
- GDPR/privacy-friendly consent & data export/delete (where required)
- Backup & restore strategy
- Fraud signals for COD / high-risk orders (basic rules)

================================================================================
13. PERFORMANCE, RELIABILITY & OPS
================================================================================
- Cloud or VPS-ready deployment
- Database backups & disaster recovery plan
- Error logging & monitoring
- Image CDN / compression
- API health checks
- Staging + production environments
- Soft delete / safe data retention for orders

================================================================================
14. INTEGRATIONS (SMB ESSENTIALS)
================================================================================
- Payment gateway adapters
- SMS gateway
- Email service (SMTP / SendGrid / etc.)
- Accounting export (or QuickBooks/Xero-ready CSV)
- Google Analytics / Meta Pixel
- Social login (Google / Facebook) — optional
- Marketplace / Facebook Shop sync — later phase

================================================================================
15. MOBILE & MULTI-CHANNEL
================================================================================
- Fully usable mobile web store
- Progressive Web App (PWA) basics (installable, offline shell)
- Admin mobile-friendly order actions (confirm, update status)
- QR code for store / product (offline-to-online)

================================================================================
16. CONTENT & SUPPORT PAGES
================================================================================
- CMS pages (About, FAQ, Privacy, Terms, Shipping Policy, Return Policy)
- Blog / tips section (optional SEO booster)
- Contact page with map / form
- Live chat widget hook (Tawk/Intercom-ready)

================================================================================
17. MULTI-STORE / BUSINESS FLEXIBILITY (MID-SIZE READY)
================================================================================
- Multi-warehouse stock (optional module)
- Multi-vendor marketplace mode (phase 2 if needed)
- B2B pricing / wholesale price lists
- Minimum order quantity & bulk pricing tiers
- Custom domain + SSL for each merchant (SaaS model)

================================================================================
18. SAAS / PLATFORM FEATURES (IF YOU SELL THE SOLUTION ITSELF)
================================================================================
- Merchant onboarding wizard
- Subscription plans (Free / Starter / Growth / Pro)
- Usage limits (products, staff, orders)
- White-label branding option
- Billing & invoice for platform fees
- Tenant isolation (data separation per store)
- Theme marketplace / template switcher

================================================================================
PRIORITY ROADMAP (RECOMMENDED BUILD ORDER)
================================================================================
Phase 1 — MVP (must ship first)
  Catalog, cart, checkout, payments (incl. COD), orders, basic admin,
  customer accounts, email notifications, responsive storefront

Phase 2 — Growth
  Coupons, reviews, analytics, abandoned cart, SMS, returns,
  bulk import, role-based staff, SEO toolkit

Phase 3 — Scale
  Multi-warehouse, B2B pricing, advanced marketing, PWA,
  courier integrations, loyalty, multi-language/currency

Phase 4 — Platform (if SaaS)
  Multi-tenant, plans/billing, onboarding, themes, white-label

================================================================================
QUALITY CHECKLIST (SERVICE STANDARDS)
================================================================================
- Checkout success rate monitored
- Order confirmation within seconds
- Stock never oversells under normal load
- Admin can fulfill an order in under 1 minute
- Mobile checkout works without zoom/horizontal scroll
- Clear return / refund policy pages published
- Daily backup verified
- Support channel for merchants documented

================================================================================
NOTES
================================================================================
- Keep the UI simple: SMB owners are not full-time store operators.
- Prefer configuration over custom code for shipping, tax, and payments.
- Local payment methods + COD matter more than exotic global gateways for many SMBs.
- Reliability and clear order status beat flashy features.
