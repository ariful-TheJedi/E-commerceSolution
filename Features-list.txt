E-COMMERCE SOLUTION — FEATURE LIST
Target: small businesses first, mid-size later.

How to read this file:
  PART 1 = the modules (each module is a separate system, built separately)
  PART 2 = checkout rules (the 8 rules that decide if you make money)
  PART 3 = local market add-on (Bangladesh / cash-on-delivery markets)
  PART 4 = build order (what to build first, second, third)
  PART 5 = not now (features to skip at launch)

################################################################################
PART 2 — CHECKOUT RULES
################################################################################
These are not features. These are rules Module 3 must obey.
Research reason: about 70% of carts are abandoned (up to 85% on mobile).
Fixing these raises sales by around 35% without changing price or product.

  RULE 1  Show the full price early.
          Shipping, tax and fees on the cart page. Never a surprise at the end.
          (This alone causes 39-48% of abandoned carts.)

  RULE 2  Never force account creation.
          Guest checkout always available. (19-26%)

  RULE 3  Keep checkout short.
          2 to 3 steps. Maximum 7 to 14 form fields. (18-22%)

  RULE 4  Show a real delivery date.
          "Arrives Thursday 24 Aug", not "3-5 business days". (16-21%)

  RULE 5  Show trust signals at payment.
          SSL badge, secure payment note, review score. (18-19%)

  RULE 6  Show the return policy before payment. (11-15%)

  RULE 7  Offer enough payment methods, including one-tap / wallet. (9-13%)

  RULE 8  Never break, never lag.
          Every 0.1 second faster = about 8.4% more sales. (12-13%)

Also: measure checkout from day one. You cannot fix a number you do not track.

PART 3 — LOCAL MARKET ADD-ON (BANGLADESH / COD MARKETS)
################################################################################
Use this part ONLY if you launch in Bangladesh or a similar market.
Reason: 70-85% of orders there are Cash on Delivery. That changes the order flow.
These are separate modules. Do not mix them into Modules 1-10.

  L1.1  bKash
  L1.2  Nagad
  L1.3  Rocket
  L1.4  Card payment (secondary, not primary)
  L1.5  Automatic payment confirmation

  Phase: 1 (if launching in BD)

################################################################################
PART 6 — RESEARCH NOTES (why the list looks like this)
################################################################################
Short version, for reference only. Not a build task.

  1. Some features are no longer "premium". In 2026 these ship as standard:
     multi-channel selling, automatic tax, API access, wallet checkout,
     semantic search, AI product text, under-2-second mobile loads.
     If you charge extra for these, your product looks old.

  2. Customers now find products through AI chat, not only Google.
     AI referral traffic to online stores grew about 4,700% in one year.
     Half of those visitors land directly on a product page.
     So: clean structured product data matters as much as SEO now.

  3. This helps small shops the most.
     75% of Shopify's AI-driven orders came from outside its top 100
     categories. Small, specific shops win here. Your target customer.

  4. Checkout is where money is lost, not the product page.
     See PART 2 for the numbers.

  5. In Bangladesh, COD is the product, not a payment option.
     Fake orders and unpaid COD returns are the biggest merchant loss.
     Local platforms compete on OTP verification, fraud flags and courier
     automation — not on catalog features.

Sources: Baymard Institute (via Stripe, Propel Commerce, Cartflows),
Salesforce State of Commerce 4th ed., Adobe AI traffic data, Shopify
Engineering, and BD platform comparisons (Uddokta, SeloraX, Dukan, BD Wallet).
